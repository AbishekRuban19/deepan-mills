Celebrating the Power of Organic Farming & Traditional Grain Purity

In the early 1980s, Mr. Kalaiazhagan, a passionate farmer, started his own small-scale business in his hometown, Theni. Initially established to produce cattle feed, Deepan Mills soon expanded into a dedicated millet manufacturing unit. For the next two decades, they manufactured millet varieties with the highest quality standards and an unwavering commitment to prompt delivery.

In 2013, Deepan Mills stepped into the National market, expanding operations with imported modern machinery and professional staffing. Today, Deepan Mills has grown to become India’s largest manufacturer of a wide spectrum of unpolished millets and traditional heritage rice varieties.

Scale, Certification & Distribution

Operating across a 3.5-acre campus with over 100 metric tonnes of millet and rice production every day, Deepan Mills is an ISO 22000:2018-certified company serving families and business partners across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh & Maharashtra.

Our Processing Standards

What Makes Deepan Mills Truly Different

Every kilogram of rice, millet, and corn passing through our Theni facility adheres to four rigorous quality commitments:

Zero Chemical Polish

Industrial mills coat grains with glucose, talc, or paraffin wax for unnatural cosmetic sheen. At Deepan Mills, our grains receive absolutely zero chemical polishing or synthetic coatings.

Low-RPM Stone De-Husking

High-speed steel rollers generate friction heat exceeding 60°C that destroys delicate B-complex vitamins. Our slow stone de-husking keeps grains cool, preserving the living bran and germ layer.

Airflow Winnowing

We separate chaff, lightweight husks, and hollow grains using natural gravity air currents, avoiding harsh chemical fumigation or sulfur bleaching common in large storage warehouses.

Natural Sun-Curing

Harvested crops and golden maize kernels are dried on clean, open-air sun yards under Tamil Nadu’s natural sunlight, locking in earthy sweetness without diesel-fired hot-air dryers.
