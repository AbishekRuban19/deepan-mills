Our Roots In Theni

We Don't Just Mill Grains. We Revive Ancient Tamil Nadu Nutrition.

Nestled in the lush agricultural heart of Theni, Tamil Nadu, Deepan Mills was established with a singular mission: to liberate Indian kitchens from polished, nutritionally void grains and bring back the vigor of our ancestors.

Unlike commercial mega-mills that employ high-friction chemical polishing to make grains look unnaturally white and shiny, we adhere strictly to slow, low-temperature stone de-husking and Vaagai wood-pressing (மரச்செக்கு). This guarantees that every grain retains its complete germ oil, natural vitamins, and medicinal fiber.

"We care about our nation’s agricultural self-reliance through environmentally conscious management. Real health begins with real, uncorrupted food."
