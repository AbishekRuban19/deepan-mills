Theni Facility & Craft

Preserving Traditional Nutritional Density Without Industrial Stripping

Situated in Theni, Tamil Nadu, along the foothills of the Western Ghats, Deepan Mills focuses on traditional post-harvest processing. We ensure native grains reach your household in their pure, unadulterated state.

Commercial processing strips the bran and germ layers to prolong shelf life at the cost of essential micronutrients. At Deepan Mills, we use low-RPM stone de-husking and traditional wooden chekkus (மரச்செக்கு) to ensure unheated extraction, zero chemical bleaching, and uncorrupted natural flavor.

"Authentic grain nutrition is defined by what remains on the grain, not what is stripped away."
