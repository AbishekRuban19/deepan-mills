Zero Spam & Confidential Customer Data Promise

Customer Privacy & Data Protection Policy

At Deepan Mills, we value the trust you place in our family agro unit. We only collect the bare minimum details required to process your grain dispatch, and we never commercialize customer data.

Effective Date: October 2026 • Deepan Mills, Theni, Tamil Nadu

Our Fundamental Privacy Principle

We only collect your name, phone number, and delivery address to weigh, pack, label, and courier your grain consignments. We do NOT run third-party advertisement trackers, we do NOT sell your contact information to data brokers, and we do NOT make automated telemarketing calls.

1

What Information We Collect

When you contact Deepan Mills or place a WhatsApp order, we may collect the following details:

  • Personal Contact Information: Your Full Name and WhatsApp/Mobile Phone Number.
  • Delivery Information: Complete Doorstep Delivery Address, Landmark, District, State, and PIN Code.
  • Order History: Product names, kilograms ordered, dates of packing, and courier tracking consignment numbers.
  • Inquiry Notes: Specific dietary preferences or custom flour grinding instructions shared by you.
Payment Security Note: We do NOT collect or store your debit card, credit card, or bank account credentials. All digital transactions occur through authorized third-party UPI gateways (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) or direct IMPS/NEFT transfers.
2

How We Use Your Personal Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate agricultural milling, packaging, and logistical fulfillment purposes:

  • To weigh, pack, label, and invoice your chosen rice, millet, or corn varieties.
  • To share live consignment docket numbers and courier tracking links via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • To communicate transit updates or verify alternative addresses in case of courier routing difficulties.
  • To provide personalized assistance regarding grain soaking and cooking ratios.
3

Authorized Logistics Partners & Zero Data Resale

Your name, phone number, and delivery address are shared solely with our verified courier and transport partners (such as ST Courier, The Professional Couriers, Delhivery, and India Post) exclusively to complete your doorstep delivery.

We never sell, lease, or monetize customer databases to advertising networks, telemarketers, or commercial brokers.
4

Your Data Protection & Deletion Rights

As a Deepan Mills customer, you have full ownership over your personal data:

  • Right to Review: You may ask our mill desk at any time what shipping details we have on file for you.
  • Right to Correct: If you change your phone number or residence, message us on WhatsApp and we will update your profile immediately.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request that your contact and address details be removed from our active customer records. Simply send a WhatsApp message stating "Delete My Record" to +91 63797 90035.
5

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding your privacy or data safety, please reach our dedicated mill desk:
